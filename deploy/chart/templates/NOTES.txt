It answers instrument questions from what it holds, and pulls from
{{ .Values.platform.address }} when it meets something it has not seen.

It does not stop working when that address does. If the platform is unreachable,
resolution continues from what is already held, and pulling resumes on its own
when the platform returns. Nothing here needs restarting for that.

Watch it:

  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -f

If it refuses to start, the message says which value is wrong. The three that
matter are the deployment identifier, the key secret and the database secret.
