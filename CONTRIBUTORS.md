# Contributors who have agreed to the contribution terms

One line per person, exactly `- @login`, added by that person in a pull request
of its own. That pull request is the record: it is made from their account, so
it is their act rather than somebody's note about them.

Employees of Societal Lab Inc. are not listed here and are not asked. Their
employment settles who owns what, and asking them to click through an agreement
as well would be theatre. The check reads GitHub's own view of who is inside the
organisation rather than a list somebody has to maintain.
